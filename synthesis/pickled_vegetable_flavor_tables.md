# Pickled Vegetable Flavor: Three Evidence-Based Tables

## Table 1. Flavor profile and major taste substances of pickled vegetables / 腌制蔬菜的风味特征及主要味觉物质

| Taste or sensory dimension / 味觉或感官维度 | Major substances or metabolite groups / 主要物质或代谢物类别 | Directly reported evidence in the supplied studies / 所供研究中的直接证据 | Source(s) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sourness / 酸味 | Lactic acid, acetic acid, and the organic-acid pool | In pickled and dried mustard (PDM), lactic and acetic acids were identified as the main organic acids; pH fell from 5.5 to 4.6 while titratable acidity rose from 3.4 to 7.1 g/kg. The paper reports four organic acids with TAV > 1 as a group; its main text does not identify all four species individually. In pickled red pepper, acetic acid was GC–O-active (threshold 210,000 µg/L; OAV 32 without biofilm and 27 with biofilm). | Jiang et al. (2026); Zhang et al. (2020) |
| Saltiness / 咸味 | Sodium chloride / measured salinity | PDM salinity was approximately 3.45% by the middle of fermentation. Pickled *Brassica napus* leaf and stem (BLS) was prepared with 4–8% added salt; the study reports salt-dependent changes in flavor and free amino acids. These are process/product measures, not TAVs for NaCl. | Jiang et al. (2026); Zhang et al. (2023) |
| Sweetness / 甜味 | Sweet-taste free amino-acid (FAA) fraction; sucrose, glucose, and fructose | In BLS, the sweet-FAA fraction was 8.14–15.02 mg/g, with the highest reported value (15.02 mg/g) in the 6%-salt group (G3). In PDM, sucrose declined and glucose/fructose rose transiently before declining; the paper does not report sugar TAVs. | Jiang et al. (2026); Zhang et al. (2023) |
| Umami / 鲜味 | L-Glutamic acid, L-aspartic acid, umami-related peptides (e.g., Pro–Glu and Glu–Lys), and reported modulators such as betaine and γ-glutamylvaline | Umami was a prominent electronic-tongue response in salted chili peppers (SCP). Glutamate, aspartate, and betaine were higher in SCP4; Pro–Glu and Glu–Lys were higher in SCP5; γ-glutamylvaline was notably higher in SCP4. In BLS, the authors identify 13 FAAs with TAV > 1 overall and highlight Asp, Glu, His, Arg, Ala, and Cys. Mature PDM is a contrast: its 17 detected FAAs were not TAV > 1. | Jiang et al. (2026); Yang et al. (2026); Zhang et al. (2023) |
| Bitterness / 苦味 | Bitter-FAA fraction; L-phenylalanine (named in SCP) | SCP5 had higher phenylalanine and stronger bitter/astringent electronic-tongue responses. In PDM, bitter FAAs dominated the FAA profile but were reported to be partly masked by salinity and acidity. The BLS study reports the most prominent bitter-FAA content in its 8%-salt group (G5). | Jiang et al. (2026); Yang et al. (2026); Zhang et al. (2023) |
| Pungency (cross-modal oral irritation) / 辛辣刺激感 | Allyl isothiocyanate (AITC), (2-isothiocyanatoethyl)benzene, and related isothiocyanates | In pickled mustard tuber, isothiocyanates accounted for 78.87% of relative peak area and sulfides for 14.48%; AITC and (2-isothiocyanatoethyl)benzene were among the major compounds associated by the authors with the characteristic hot/pungent flavor. The paper states that more than 60 days of pickling was needed for typical aroma notes. This is a pungency/aroma observation, not a basic-taste TAV. | Jiang et al. (2026); Liu et al. (2009) |

**Table 1 note.** Product-specific results are shown; they should not be treated as universal concentrations for all pickled vegetables. PDM = pickled and dried mustard; BLS = *Brassica napus* leaf and stem; SCP = salted chili pepper; G1–G5 denote the BLS treatments with 4%, 5%, 6%, 7%, and 8% added salt, respectively. TAV = taste activity value.

| L-Aspartic acid / L-天冬氨酸 | Umami / 鲜味 | Identified as an umami amino acid in SCP; its level was higher in SCP4 than in the other sampled origins. | Yang et al. (2026) |

| γ-Glutamyl peptides / γ-谷氨酰肽类 | Umami-related peptides / 鲜味相关肽 | Reported among the umami-peptide classes in the metabolomic profile; the main text does not provide a complete compound-by-compound list for this class. | Yang et al. (2026) |

| Betaine / 甜菜碱 | Umami enhancement (author interpretation) / 鲜味增强（作者解释） | Elevated in SCP4; the authors infer that it contributes to SCP4’s umami characteristics. | Yang et al. (2026) |
| Reduced L-glutathione / 还原型 L-谷胱甘肽 | Umami enhancement / 鲜味增强 | Reported at a uniquely high level in SCP5; the authors discuss its likely modulatory contribution to taste. | Yang et al. (2026) |
| L-Phenylalanine / L-苯丙氨酸 | Bitter / 苦味 | Identified as a bitter amino acid; its level was significantly higher in SCP5 than in the other four samples. | Yang et al. (2026) |

**Table 2 note.** In mature PDM, 17 FAAs were detected, but the authors report that none reached TAV > 1; this study therefore does not support listing those FAAs as key taste-active compounds at maturity (Jiang et al., 2026). The SCP metabolomics paper labels its features “umami-related differential metabolites”; its named metabolites are retained as study-reported candidates/modulators rather than treated as universally validated taste actives. TAV values and concentrations appear only where explicitly available in the cited study text.

**Table 3 note.** NPRP = pickled red pepper without biofilm; FPRP = pickled red pepper with biofilm; SCP = salted chili pepper; PDM = pickled and dried mustard; BLS = *Brassica napus* leaf and stem. FD = flavor-dilution factor; OAV = odor activity value; ROAV = relative odor activity value; OAC = odor-active compound. Thresholds for the red-pepper study are shown in the source units (µg/L); OAVs are unitless and are listed in NPRP/FPRP order. No conversion to mg/kg was made. “Not detected” and “not stated” are kept distinct. The PDM study also reports high ROAVs for dimethyl disulfide, carbon disulfide, and methanethiol, but its main text does not provide compound-level odor descriptors; no descriptors are inferred here. A separate GC–O study of Sichuan paocai identified shared skeleton odorants including phenylethyl alcohol, acetic acid, butanoic acid, 2-methylbutanoic acid, ethyl hexanoate, 4-ethylphenol, and 4-ethylguaiacol; because compound-level descriptors and threshold values are not supplied in its abstract, those data are not assigned to individual rows here (Zhao et al., 2021).
